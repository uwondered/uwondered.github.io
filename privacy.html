Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how U Wondered ("we", "us") handles information on this website and in U Wondered Studio, the internal tool we use to publish our own videos.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not ask you for any personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

U Wondered Studio

U Wondered Studio is an application used only by U Wondered. It connects to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok through their official APIs in order to upload videos to our own accounts and to read statistics about our own content. It is not offered to the public and does not access accounts that belong to anyone else.

When we authorize the application on a platform, it receives:

  • basic profile information of our own account, such as the account name, ID and profile picture;
  • permission to upload and publish videos to our own account;
  • statistics about our own videos, such as views, likes and comments counts.

This information is used only to publish our videos and understand how they perform. Access tokens and data are stored on our own computer, are never sold, and are never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings.

YouTube

U Wondered Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it with YouTube, we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Authorization can be revoked at Google security settings.

Instagram and TikTok

Data received from Instagram is handled under the Instagram Privacy Policy. Data received from TikTok is handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

To ask about or request deletion of any data related to U Wondered, email uwonderedchannel@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
